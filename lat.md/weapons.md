# Weapons and Physics Layers

This document outlines the design and integration of the weapon systems and physics collision configurations.

## Weapon Manager
<!-- lat: { "require-code-mention": true } -->
The `WeaponManager` resides under the player's camera:
* Resolves the list of active weapons from player inventories.
* Automatically sorts weapon instances from weakest to strongest.
* Spawns projectile nodes and handles hitscan raycasting.

## Weapon Resources
Defines the custom resource configuration files that govern weapon attributes.

* Individual weapon stats are defined via `WeaponData` resource files (`.tres` and `scripts/weapons/weapon_data.gd`) located under `assets/weapons/` across 14 distinct weapons (`GameState.WEAPON_ORDER`).
* Modifications to ammo capacity, reload speed, damage, and projectile patterns should be edited in these resource files.
* Every imported gun is held by gloved hands (`ViewmodelHands`, `scripts/weapons/viewmodel_hands.gd`, node `Hands` under the viewmodel, `Weapon.hands`). `ViewmodelHands.grip_points` reads the grip off the gun's own vertices (the lowest ones in the rear half) and, for guns at least `TWO_HAND_LEN` long, a support point on the underside of the fore-end; a shorter gun is held two-handed too, the support hand wrapped round the trigger hand thumbs-forward (`_build_cup_hand`). `_apply_real_model` first pushes the gun forward until its grip is at `GRIP_Z`, so the trigger hand is inside the frame. The hands scale with the gun's height (`hand_scale`), cast no shadow, and are left out of `rim_meshes()`. The forearms run to fixed elbows (`ELBOW_RIGHT` / `ELBOW_LEFT`) outside the view at hip and ADS. The support hand and its forearm hang off a pivot at the left elbow (`support_arm`): `Weapon._reload_beat` swings it down off the gun on the mag-out / vent / breech beat (`support_off`), back on the mag-in / charge / load beat (`support_on`), dips it per shell (`support_dip`), and `_finish_reload` always ends with it on the gun. The trigger hand's index finger hangs off two joints (`trigger_finger`, `trigger_joint`, tip marker `trigger_tip`): `Weapon.try_fire` calls `squeeze()` every frame the trigger is down and not reloading, and the hands curl it over `PULL_TIME`, hold at least `PULL_HOLD` and release over `RELEASE_TIME` (`tests/trigger_pull_probe`). `tests/viewmodel_hands_probe`; studio look tool `tools/hands_shot.tscn` (windowed: eye and side view of every gun).
* Gunshot muzzle flashes (`scenes/fx/muzzle_flash.tscn`, `scripts/fx/muzzle_flash.gd`, shader `shaders/muzzle_flash.gdshader`) are three additive cards: a front star across the bore (4-7 petals, new count and roll every shot) and two prongs along the barrel. `Weapon._play_muzzle` sets `tint_color`, `size_mult` and `along_bore`. Without `along_bore`, as on the enemies (their muzzle nodes face anywhere), the flash is one camera-facing star. The star never grows past `STAR_MAX` of its size, so a big gun's flash close to the eye stays under 16 deg. `tests/muzzle_flash_probe`; `tools/hands_shot.tscn -- --fire` freezes each gun's flash at full burn.
* Tracers (`scenes/fx/tracer.tscn`, `tracer_red.tscn`, `scripts/fx/tracer.gd`, shader `shaders/tracer_streak.gdshader`) are one flat card along the line of fire that `_face_camera` rolls toward the viewport's camera every frame: a white-hot core in a halo of the shot colour, brightest at the nose and fading out at the tail. The scene's cylinder only sets the width (`CARD_SPREAD` times its diameter). The spill light comes from the FXLights budget. `tests/tracer_streak_probe`.
* Imported guns are skinned by `Weapon._gunmetal_material`: albedo from the kit times the gun's `REAL_MODELS` tint, metallic 0.88, and roughness 0.52 times a shared noise map ramped to 0.7..1 (so 0.36..0.52, never a mirror), plus a faint fine-grain normal map (`_gunmetal_grain`). The kit's saturated surfaces become emissive accents (`_energy_accent_material`). `tests/gun_finish_probe`.
* Every gun builds a holographic round counter (`Weapon._build_ammo_readout`, node `AmmoReadout` under the viewmodel) off `_fit_box`, the fitted model's bounds from `_apply_real_model`: left flank, above the top, at most `READOUT_REACH` forward of the stock. It refreshes on `ammo_changed` / `reload_started` / `_finish_reload` and turns `READOUT_LOW_COLOR` with a pulse while `readout_low()`. `tests/ammo_readout_probe`.
* Reloads follow `WeaponData.reload_style` (MAG, SHELLS, CELL, TUBE): `Weapon._play_reload_anim` tweens the viewmodel into the style's `RELOAD_POSES` entry and back across the reload time, and `reload_beats()` schedules the beats (jolt + sound + the `reload_beat` signal) in between. CELL guns get a glowing `EnergyCell` strip (`_build_energy_cell`), whose material joins `_accent_mats`; the vent beat drains those to near dark and the charge beat refills them to `ACCENT_ENERGY`. `tests/reload_anim_probe`.
* Standing still, the WeaponHolder breathes (`WeaponManager`, `BREATH_*` constants): a slow figure-eight of about 2.6 mm and 0.26 deg pitch each way, one breath every 3.7 s. It fades out while moving (the walk bob takes over), drops to `BREATH_ADS` while aiming, and grows to `BREATH_WINDED` times as large and `BREATH_WINDED_RATE` times as fast as stamina runs out (the shooter's `stamina_changed`). `tests/idle_breath_probe`.
* Feel lives there too. The "Kick Feel" group is each gun's recoil signature in the hands: `kick_back` / `kick_lift` / `kick_climb` / `kick_roll` / `kick_roll_bias` / `kick_side` are the per-shot velocity impulse `WeaponManager._on_fired` gives the viewmodel, and `kick_stiffness` / `kick_damping` are the spring that settles it (clamped to `KICK_MAX_STIFFNESS` / `KICK_MAX_DAMPING`). `fov_punch` widens the view through `Player.fov_punch`, `fire_thump` layers the `fire_thump` sub-bass under the report and scales the rumble, `action_clack` plays `action_clack` a beat after it. Every gun has its own `sound_id` (`<id>_fire` in `SoundSynth`; the Tesla beam plays it per tick). `tests/weapon_feel_probe`.

## Weapon Mods
<!-- lat: { "require-code-mention": true } -->
Bought in the Armory, a mod changes what a gun's hit does, not how hard it lands. One mod per hitscan or beam gun, refitted for free.

* `GameState.MOD_DEFS`, `owned_mods`, `weapon_mods` (scene path -> mod id), saved with the run and cleared by `start_campaign`. `BASE_LOADOUT` must match `player.tscn`'s rack (`tests/weapon_mods_probe` checks).
* `WeaponMods` runs the effect from `Weapon._do_hitscan` and `_update_beam`: CHAIN ARC (35% to the nearest robot within 7 m), THERMITE (30% of every hit over 3 s, six ticks; one burn per robot that each hit adds to and restarts, ticks ignore armour), RICOCHET (a wall hit bounces 60% into a robot within 15 m and 35 degrees of the reflection), OVERRIDE (a hit leaving a non-boss below 25% hijacks it for 6 s, 8 s cooldown per gun).
* ARC and RICOCHET proc at most once per 120 ms per gun, so shotgun pellets and beam ticks draw one arc or bounce. The limit thins the effect, not the damage: `WeaponMods._bank` holds the hits in between and the next proc carries them, so a fast gun gets its full share. THERMITE is not limited.
* `tests/mod_value_probe` (headless report) measures each mod's damage per gun against an unarmoured and an armour-4 pack.
* Proc damage goes through `GameState.apply_secondary_damage`, which pays score, leech and OVERLOAD charge but is not a new hit for accuracy or the AI Director. Breakable-cover shrapnel uses it too.
* `WeaponMods` and `GameState.mod_compatible` duck-type robots and weapons: naming `EnemyBase` or `Weapon` there closes a load cycle through the pickup scenes and `pickup.gd` fails to load.

## Damage Matchups
<!-- lat: { "require-code-mention": true } -->
Every gun deals a damage class and every robot wears an armour class; the pair scales each player weapon hit (`scripts/weapons/damage_matrix.gd`).

* `WeaponData.damage_class`: KINETIC (pistol, rifle, .50), SHRED (Breacher, CX-4 Cascade), THERMAL (plasma, OMEGA), ARC (Tesla, Arc Coil, Tempest), EXPLOSIVE (Devastator, Swarm), RAIL (Longshot, Gauss). `DamageMatrix.ARMOUR` maps every `EnemyBase.codex_key` to CHASSIS (neutral), PLATED, LIGHT or NETWORKED; `TABLE` holds the multipliers (0.75 to 1.6). Bosses in `BOSSES` take the swing at `BOSS_DAMP` (half).
* Applied once in `Damageable.apply_damage`, before the owner's `modify_incoming_damage` and flat armour, from `hit_weapon` (set by `KillFx.tag`), so hitscan, beam ticks, projectile hits and splash all pass through it and untagged damage (grenades, melee, hazards, mod procs) never does. The multiplier the last hit took is kept on `Damageable.matchup`.
* Feedback: a counter hit (>= `WEAK_AT`) draws a cyan damage number, a resisted one (<= `RESIST_AT`) a small steel one; both reach `GameState.report_matchup`, which emits `weapon_matchup`, plays `hit_weak` / `hit_resist` (one per `MATCHUP_CUE_GAP_MS`) and teaches each armour class's `MATCHUP_HINTS` line once per run.
* The robot reacts to what hit it: `EnemyBase._on_damaged` reads `hp.hit_weapon` (still tagged inside the emitting `apply_damage`) into `_hit_class`, and `_play_hit_flash` tints the overlay `CLASS_COLORS[class]` (ARC stutters twice, THERMAL fades over 0.34 s, the rest 0.14 s; untagged white). Poise is scaled by `poise_mult(hp.matchup)` (`POISE_WEAK` 1.35, `POISE_RESIST` 0.7), so the right gun staggers a robot sooner than its damage alone would. `tests/hit_reaction_probe`.
* Walls too: `Weapon._spawn_impact` calls `Impact.set_damage_class` after `set_surface` (RAIL cyan sparks and light, ARC blue-white, SHRED half the sparks per pellet; KINETIC keeps the surface colours) and `BulletMark.spawn` takes the class (RAIL: a 1.4x hole with a glowing rim that cools over `RAIL_COOL`; ARC: a blue scorch glow over `ARC_COOL`). `tests/impact_class_probe`, `tests/impact_class_shot`.
* The enemy codex and the HUD field manual compute "effective weapons" from the table (`DamageMatrix.codex_lines`, `counters_text`); `EnemyCodex` entries no longer author a weapons list. The weapon codex shows each gun's `describe_class`. `tests/weapon_matchup_probe`.

## Cascade
<!-- lat: { "require-code-mention": true } -->
The CX-4 Cascade's kills chain-detonate through a pack (`scripts/weapons/cascade.gd`): the early horde-clearer, ranked between the Breacher and the .50 and first offered by level 2's spawn.

* A gun with `WeaponData.cascade_damage` > 0 primes every robot its hitscan hits (`Cascade.prime`, before the hit lands so the killing round counts): a `CascadeCharge` child with a cyan pilot light and a hexagonal `fuse` ring (a 6-ring TorusMesh, `FUSE_RADIUS`) that spins and blinks with `fuse_period()`, `FUSE_BLINK.x` fresh down to `FUSE_BLINK.y` at expiry, hidden once the prime lapses, live for `PRIME_TIME` (2.5 s) and refreshed by each hit. The blast scales with the Armory damage track (`eff_damage() / damage`).
* A primed robot that dies to anything (grenade, mod proc, hazard) detonates `POP_DELAY` later via its `Damageable.died`: everything hostile within `cascade_radius` is primed one generation deeper at `DECAY` (0.8x) and then takes the blast through `GameState.apply_secondary_damage`; `Cascade._link_fx` draws a jagged arc (an `ArcBolt` of `LINK_SEGS` links, faded out over `LINK_TIME`) from the blast to each robot it primes. Victims it kills detonate in turn; `MAX_GEN` caps the depth. Only the `enemy` group is hit, so the player never is; bosses (`BOSS_HP`) are never primed.
* Explosive props in range (`destructible` group with `Destructible.explode`: barrels, gas canisters, cars) take `PROP_MULT` x the blast, so they always go off, and their own splash (which can hurt the player, like any barrel) kills and so pops more primed robots. Crates and fences are left standing.
* A chain of 2+ pops reports through `GameState.note_cascade` once `SETTLE` passes with no new pop: `cascade_chain(pops, pos)` drives the HUD stamp (`CASCADE xN`, `STACK OVERFLOW xN` from 6), chains of `CASCADE_CALLOUT` (3) or more pay `CASCADE_BONUS` per robot past the second and teach the `cascade` hint, and `stat_best_cascade` lands in the debrief highlights. `tests/cascade_probe`.

## Salvage
<!-- lat: { "require-code-mention": true } -->
Heavy robots drop their own gun for the player to spend (`scripts/weapons/salvage.gd`).

* `Salvage.TABLE` maps a `codex_key` to a salvage weapon scene: GUNNER -> SIEGE Chaingun (120 rounds, 14/s, pierce 1), MECH -> PALM Cannon (16 plasma bolts with splash). Neither is in `GameState.WEAPON_ORDER`: no codex page, no pickup pacing, never carried into the next level.
* `EnemyBase._ready` connects `hp.died` to `Salvage.drop_from` (override-proof). `drop_at` spawns `weapon_pickup.tscn` as `Pickup.Kind.SALVAGE` (set through `SALVAGE_KIND`, duck-typed: naming `Pickup` from robot code closes the pickup load cycle); untaken, it blinks for `BLINK_AT` s and is gone after `LIFETIME` (25 s).
* `WeaponManager.take_salvage` arms it at once with a full magazine and 0 reserve; one at a time (a new one tosses the old). `Weapon.salvage` makes `add_ammo` a no-op and keeps `_equip` from writing `GameState.equipped_weapon`. When a shot leaves it empty, `_toss_salvage` removes it, re-arms the gun held before (`_pre_salvage`), emits `weapon_removed` (the HUD rebuilds its carousel) and resets the watchdog's rack count so the removal is not reported as a lost weapon. `tests/salvage_probe`.

## Kill Styles
<!-- lat: { "require-code-mention": true } -->
The weapon that lands the killing blow picks how a robot dies (`WeaponData.kill_fx`, `scripts/fx/kill_fx.gd`).

* DISINTEGRATE (gauss, Longshot, plasma, OMEGA): the robot's materials swap to `shaders/dissolve.gdshader`, which burns it away top-down in 1 s behind a glowing edge; no wreck. ELECTROCUTE (tesla, arc coil, tempest): 0.75 s of spasms under arcs and a blue skin, then the classic blast (`EnemyBase._classic_death_fx`). SHRED (Breacher shotgun, .50 magnum): `KillFx.shred` throws the robot 3.2 m away from the shooter (`EnemyBase._shot_dir`) in a tumbling arc with a scrap spray, a world ray cutting the flight short of walls, then the classic blast where it lands. DECAPITATE: tagged by `Weapon._do_hitscan` itself (not WeaponData) on a headshot from a gun with `kill_fx` 0 (pistol, rifle); `EnemyBase._decapitate` folds the head bone (`_head_bone`), flings a head chunk with its eye lit, fountains the neck (`KillFx.neck_fountain`) and rocks the chassis back for `DECAP_TIME` before the classic blast. No head bone, or a drone/raptor: the ordinary death. BLAST (Devastator, Swarm Launcher): `EnemyBase._blast_apart` tears up to `BLAST_LIMBS` limbs and the head off at once (`_dismember_limb(..., force)` past `LIMB_LOSS_MAX`), lofts the torso `BLAST_LIFT` m through two chain-reaction pops over `BLAST_TIME`, then the classic blast; drones and raptors detonate mid-air.
* The style rides on `Damageable.kill_fx` only for the duration of the weapon's `apply_damage` call (`KillFx.tag` / `untag`). `died` fires inside that call, so `_on_died` sees the killing hit's style and nothing else: grenades and hazards never tag.
* Bosses keep their deaths. Overrides of `_on_died` opt in through `EnemyBase._kill_style()` / `_prep_kill_fx()`: drones and raptors burn away mid-air or spasm before they fall, menders and skitters split like the base. A SHRED kill knocks drones and raptors along the shot as they fall (`EnemyBase._shred_kick`). The seeker keeps its shot-down blast, a mechanic. `tests/kill_fx_probe`.

## Data Bleed
<!-- lat: { "require-code-mention": true } -->
Robots bleed binary when hit and pour it out when they die (`scripts/fx/data_bleed.gd`).

* `DataBleed.spill` runs from `Weapon._enemy_hit_pop` on every hitscan hit on a robot (`hit_amount`: `HIT_MIN`..`HIT_MAX` by damage, +`CRIT_BONUS` on a crit), thrown back toward the shooter; walls never spill. At most `FRAME_BUDGET` spills start per physics frame (shotgun pellets).
* `DataBleed.burst` runs from `EnemyBase._data_burst`, on `hp.died` rather than in `_on_died`, so subclasses that override `_on_died` without super still burst: `BURST_AMOUNT` glyphs rising from mid-body. It ignores the frame budget.
* One CPUParticles3D per spill, freeing itself on `finished`; a two-frame sheet ("0" | "1", `glyph_texture()` drawn from 5x7 bitmaps) picked per particle by `anim_offset`. Unshaded, `disable_fog`, no shadows; LOW halves the amounts. `tests/data_bleed_probe`.

## Weapon Signatures
<!-- lat: { "require-code-mention": true } -->
Every gun shares one flash, tracer, impact and smoke, tinted and sized by its data; a signature is the firing FX that belong to one gun alone (`scripts/weapons/fx/`).

* `WeaponData.signature_fx` names a script extending `SignatureFx` (`scripts/weapons/fx/signature_fx.gd`, a RefCounted); `Weapon._ready` builds one per gun with `SignatureFx.make` (a silent default when the data names none, so an unsigned gun fires as before). The weapon is duck-typed as a `Node3D` there so the two scripts never name each other's class (a load cycle nilled autoloads on the enemies).
* Hooks, called from the firing path: `tracer_style` (asked once per round: overrides for the tracer's `bolt_speed`/`bolt_length`/`bolt_width` and a `color`, applied in `Weapon._spawn_tracer`; a RAMPAGE tier's `shot_color` beats the colour), `owns_muzzle_smoke` (skips `Weapon._muzzle_smoke`, which tags its node `shared_fx = muzzle_smoke`), `owns_brass` (skips `Weapon._eject_brass`, tagged `shared_fx = brass`), `on_fire` (after the shared muzzle FX in `Weapon._do_shot`), `on_world_hit` and `on_enemy_hit` (from `Weapon._do_hitscan`, after the bullet hole / hit pop, both with the surface normal), `on_process` (every frame from `Weapon._process`, after `_update_heat`), `on_beam_tick` (each damage tick of a BEAM gun in `Weapon._update_beam`, with the robot or collider struck), `on_projectile` (after `launch` in `Weapon._spawn_projectile`). Signature nodes carry a `sig_fx` meta naming the effect, which the probes filter on.
* M9 Sidearm, `SidearmFx` (`scripts/weapons/fx/sidearm_fx.gd`): bolt `BOLT` (1.1 m at 240 m/s); a round stopped on a wall ricochets with `RICOCHET_CHANCE` per surface (metal 1, concrete/stone 0.7, soft 0) as the tracer scene in bolt mode along `dir.bounce(normal)` jittered up to `RICOCHET_JITTER_DEG`, never back into the wall, with the `ricochet` synth whine (`SoundSynth._ricochet`); a round on a robot throws `_glint`, two crossed billboard bars that fade in `GLINT_TIME`; `_breech_wisp` rises from the ejection port in place of the shared smoke. `tests/sidearm_fx_probe`.
* AR-7 Pulse Rifle, `PulseRifleFx` (`scripts/weapons/fx/pulse_rifle_fx.gd`): counts rounds in `tracer_style`, every `TRACER_EVERY`th is `LONG` (3.2 m, tinted toward white) and the rest `SHORT` beads; `_update_haze` (from `on_process`) builds one `shaders/heat_haze.gdshader` curtain quad over the muzzle once `Weapon._heat` passes `HAZE_HEAT`, shown with `strength` following the heat and hidden when cool; `_streamer` replaces the shared smoke with `STREAMER_MIN + heat * STREAMER_PER_HEAT` particles; `_spall` (from `on_enemy_hit`) sprays darts along the round's travel projected onto the struck face. `tests/rifle_fx_probe`.
* SG-12 Breacher, `BreacherFx` (`scripts/weapons/fx/breacher_fx.gd`): `tracer_style` returns `PELLET` beads or, while `Weapon._alt_slug`, one `SLUG` bolt; `on_fire` spawns `_bloom` (an additive cone under the muzzle), `_wad` (one tumbling disc out the bore at `WAD_SPEED`), `_rolling_smoke` (in place of the shared puff) and `_hull_later` (the hull `HULL_DELAY` after the shot, the pump beat, in place of the shared brass); `on_enemy_hit` throws `_pepper` once per blast (hits within `PEPPER_WINDOW_MS` of the last are skipped). `tests/shotgun_fx_probe`.
* CX-4 Cascade, `CascadeFx` (`scripts/weapons/fx/cascade_fx.gd`): `BOLT` dart; `on_enemy_hit` stamps `_tag`, a 6-ring hexagon laid on the struck face that snaps from 2x and fades in `TAG_TIME`. The prime's fuse ring and the chain's link arcs live in `Cascade` (see Cascade), since chain-primed robots wear them too. `tests/cascade_fx_probe`.
* .50 Maelstrom, `MaelstromFx` (`scripts/weapons/fx/maelstrom_fx.gd`): `BOLT` streak with a hotter `color`; `on_fire` spawns `_port_jets` (two additive cones standing up from the barrel top, `PORT_SPACING` apart along the bore, behind the tip) and `_smoke_ring` (a TorusMesh vortex ring, axis down the line of fire, tweened forward, up, to `RING_GROW` and transparent over `RING_LIFE`; in place of the shared puff); `on_enemy_hit` throws `_exit_spray` `EXIT_DEPTH` past the hit along the round; `on_world_hit` throws `_crater`, a node whose local +Y is the surface normal holding a flat (`flatness` 1, local-coords) `DustRing` and a burst of chunks. `tests/magnum_fx_probe`.
* VK-7 Tesla Projector, `TeslaFx` (`scripts/weapons/fx/tesla_fx.gd`): `on_beam_tick` on a robot adds (or recharges) one `TeslaFx.Cage` child named `CAGE_NAME`, which measures the robot's merged visual AABB in its frame and every frame re-zaps `CAGE_ARCS` `ArcBolt`s between random points on that box's faces, with a budgeted flickering light; `strength()` falls from 1 to 0 over `RESIDUAL` after the last tick, lighting fewer arcs, then the cage frees itself.
* `ArcBolt` (`scripts/fx/arc_bolt.gd`): a chain of top-level box segments in one shared additive material; `zap(a, b, amp)` lays them along a fresh jagged polyline (kinks up to `amp`, most mid-span), `set_alpha` fades it, `points()` returns the segment ends. Thin geometry rather than an ImmediateMesh line, which draws one pixel wide at any distance.

## Rail Trail
<!-- lat: { "require-code-mention": true } -->
Railgun rounds ionize the air they cross (`scripts/fx/rail_trail.gd`, `WeaponData.rail_trail`: the gauss and the Longshot).

* `Weapon._do_hitscan` calls `RailTrail.spawn` from the muzzle to where the round stopped, in `shot_color()` (so a RAMPAGE tier tints it). The trail starts `START` past the muzzle and keeps at most `MAX_LEN`; at most `MAX_LIVE` live at once, the oldest freed first.
* One ArrayMesh per shot (`build_mesh`): a helix band `PITCH` metres a turn at `RADIUS` (vertex `NORMAL` points out from the axis, `COLOR.a` 0) round two crossed core quads (`COLOR.a` 1). `shaders/rail_trail.gdshader` animates it from the `age` instance uniform over `LIFE`: the coil swells, wobbles and rises like smoke as it fades, the core burns out in the first half. Additive, fog-free, shadowless; LOW uses fewer segments a turn. `tests/rail_trail_probe`.

## Rampage Charge
The gun charges up with the RAMPAGE kill streak (`Weapon._on_rampage_changed`, on `GameState.rampage_changed`).

* At tier n, `Weapon.shot_color()` returns `GameState.RAMPAGE_COLORS[n - 1]` (the HUD banner's palette, one source): tracers and the muzzle flash take it, and the flash grows `RAMPAGE_FLASH_GROW` per tier.
* Every viewmodel mesh outside the muzzle subtree (`rim_meshes()`) wears `assets/materials/rampage_rim.tres` (`shaders/rampage_rim.gdshader`, a pulsing fresnel rim) as `material_overlay`; tier 0 takes it off. `tests/weapon_rampage_probe`.

## Grenades
Defines the physics layers and player-bound scripts for throwing grenades.

* Unlike standard firearm classes, Grenades live directly on the player script (`grenade_kinds` in `scripts/player/player.gd`) and are mapped to physics layer 8.
* Four grenade types are supported: FRAG, VORTEX (singularity gravity-well), EMP (disables robot firmware), and HIJACK (converts robot loyalty).
* Grenade actions are mapped to `grenade` (throw, `G`) and `grenade_cycle` (cycle types, `H`).

## Collision Mask Configuration
<!-- lat: { "require-code-mention": true } -->
Collision layers determine raycast and physics interaction target filtering:
* **Enemy Hitscans & Line of Sight:** Raycast masks target Layer 1 (World) and Layer 2 (Player).
* **Player Projectiles & Fire:** Raycast masks target Layer 1 (World) and Layer 4 (Enemies).
