# Changelog

All notable changes to this project will be documented in this file.

## [Unreleased]

### Fixed
- **Heavy guns no longer paint a disc over the screen when they fire.** The air-pressure ring thrown by the Breacher, .50, Longshot, Gauss, Plasma, Swarm, Tempest, Devastator and OMEGA grew 6x where it spawned, 0.1 m off the muzzle, so for 0.18 s every shot covered 27-39 deg of the view with an opaque orange or blue donut. It now forms 0.5 m past the muzzle, flies 3 m down the line of fire as it widens and fades in 0.2 s, never wider than 7.6 deg. `muzzle_ring_probe` (suite); new windowed look tool `viewmodel_shot` photographs every gun in hand at rest and at the kick's peak, which is how it was found.
- **You can walk up grok's first tower corner and horde's sky-bridge.** Two things stopped the player dead on a ramp with no jump (#339). Grok's two towers stood 2.2 m too close, so the 7 m tower's second landing hung 1.7 m over the 8 m tower's first and you hit your head on the first corner; the 7 m tower moved 2.8 m north. And a ramp starting up on a deck tapered to a point, a 48 deg spike the player walked into (horde's sky-bridge stopped 3 of 3 walks); a deck foot is now full width, except where a switchback's flight starts over the last one. `tower_spacing_probe` (suite). The report tool `tests/ramp_walk_triage` now keeps the walker in god mode from the first frame (7 of level 01's 11 "stuck" ramps were a dead walker, and all 22 on horde and frostbreak), seeds each level, and walks a stopped ramp 3 times, marking only a stop on every try as REPEATABLE, since seeded passes still differ. `ramp_probe` is seeded too, so two runs match ramp for ramp.
- The ramp walker never switched robots off. `tests/ramp_walker.gd` looked them up in group `enemies`, but robots join `enemy`, so in every `ramp_walk_triage` pass so far the level's robots stayed live and shoved, blocked and knocked back the walker: part of why the same ramp walked clean in one pass and stopped in the next (#339). `house_stairs_probe` (suite) now checks that every robot is off during the walks.
- **The game-over screen is translated.** The overlord's parting shot, the "Taken down by" line, the tip for whatever killed you and the restart button showed in English in every language (38 strings). They now have rows, the lines built around them translate first, and the strings check fails CI on any new taunt or tip without a row (#337). `death_screen_i18n_probe` (suite).
- **You can climb the stairs in every enterable house.** The interior ramp ran under the upper floor for its first 2.5 m, so in Maple Grove's four houses you walked into the ceiling 1.25 m up and could not climb without jumping. In level 01's two houses the ramp was 49 and 56 deg, too steep to walk. The ramp now runs the whole back wall to a landing, with the floor open above it wherever your head would hit: 30 deg in Maple Grove, 39 and 42 deg in level 01 (its taller house is now 10 m, not 12). Robots path up them too (#290). `house_stairs_probe` (suite).
- **No more floodlight masts through bridges and towers.** An outdoor light's mast was built before the level's ramps and bridges existed, so 34 of 126 masts in 14 levels went straight through something solid. The worst were the SkyBridges in Skybridge Uplink, the Mind Cathedral and titan's arena, where a pole in the middle of the deck stopped you dead. A mast whose column is not clear is now left out (#290). `pylon_clearance_probe` (suite).
- The probe suite no longer passes a probe that hit a script error. A runtime error aborts the rest of the coroutine it happens in, so a probe could print `RESULT PASS` with most of its checks never run. Both runners (`tools/run_tests.sh`, `tools/run_tests.ps1`) now fail any probe whose output contains `SCRIPT ERROR`, and say so. A census of all 166 suite probes found none that errors today (#267).
- `returner_probe` failed "its marker is an upright ring" whenever the machine fell behind (every run at `--max-fps 10`): it checked the elite marker 10 physics frames after spawning, but the marker is built 4 process frames in, and a loaded machine runs several physics frames per process frame. It now waits for the marker itself (#338).
- **The story is translated.** The comic briefing's taglines and trace log, the act headers, the SECTOR CLEARED lead lines, the credits' 03:14 recap and every overlord line (126 strings) showed in English in Spanish, French, German and Portuguese. All now have rows, and the act header, trace card header and lead line, which were built in a way no translation could match, translate first. The strings check fails CI on any new story line without a row (#236). `story_i18n_probe` (suite).
- **The lens flare works on Maple Grove's photographic skies.** Maple Grove Estates and Maple Grove Plaza use a real sky photo, and their sun is painted into it, so nothing told the flare where it was and looking at it did nothing. Each now carries the sun's measured direction (`hdri_sun_dir`, found by `tools/hdri_sun_dir.gd`), so the flare blooms on the sun in the photo and fades behind the skyline like everywhere else (#234). `lens_flare_probe` (suite), `lens_flare_shot` (windowed).
- New suite probe `cull_aabb_probe` pins every robot's culling box to the size of the robot (#207). The 50-300 m boxes reported for eight skinned robots were their imported mesh AABBs; culling has used the clamped `custom_aabb` since July (gunner: 298 m imported, 3.2 m culled), so they already frustum-culled. With the clamp disabled the probe fails 10 robots at 18-298 m.
- `wreck_physics_probe` could fail on CI with "gunner wreck comes to rest" (#302). It looked for two still samples 0.1 s apart in process time, but the catwalk gunner settles in 1.5-2.2 game seconds and sometimes hits the 2.2 s tumble cap still moving, then goes straight into its sink. The probe now takes the wreck's transform when the wreck itself settles, waited on physics frames, and prints the settle time and whether it slept or hit the cap.
- `gun_range_probe` could fail on a loaded CI runner with one shotgun blast's worth of decals (9 of the 10 wanted): it fired on a fixed game-time gap, and a pull the gun refused because its cooldown had not run out was silently dropped. It now retries a refused pull for up to 1.5 s of game time and checks that both blasts fired (#202).
- **Mid-level surprise toasts are translated.** The ASSASSIN CONTRACT, SUPPLY FLARE, GRID SURGE and MODEL HALLUCINATION callouts, the purge countdown, the payload, prompt-injector and vision-scanner toasts showed in English in every language (27 strings). Three of them put their number into the English text before translating, so no translation could ever have matched; they now translate first. The strings check fails CI on any new toast without a row (#331). `teach_hint_i18n_probe` (suite).
- **Coaching hints are translated.** The one-time hints that explain a trap, an elite affix or a new move the first time you meet it showed in English in every language. All 33 now have Spanish, French, German and Portuguese text and reach the screen translated, and the strings check fails CI on any new hint without a row. `teach_hint_i18n_probe` (suite).
- **Robots climb ramps from any side.** A ramp rising from the ground used to taper to a point at its foot, leaving knee-high side walls either side of the tip. You never noticed, because your step-up climbs them, but a robot approaching off the centre line walked into them and stopped: an android chasing you over Maple Grove's canal bridge sat at the foot of the approach for 20 s. Ground-level ramp feet are now full width (and so are feet up on a tower corner or a deck, except a switchback's: see the tower entry above, #339). Maple Grove's two bridge approaches now land on the deck's edge instead of 0.4 m short of it, which left a 12 cm lip robots could not climb. `ramp_foot_probe` (suite).

### Changed
- **The build is 17.7 MB lighter.** 14 models nothing loads any more still shipped in every export, because the export packs every resource: the first K-9 model (the game uses `robot_dog_v2`), an unused fishbot fork, the bladed flyer the RAPTOR replaced, four cars, five blasters no weapon uses, and the health-pack model reverted in June together with the three textures only it used. Shipped imports go from 271.4 to 253.7 MB, and `tools/check_import_budget.py` now fails CI on any shipped model that no scene, script or resource names, with its budget ratcheted from 272 to 255 MB (#56).
- **Dead robots go limp.** A killed robot used to tumble as a stiff statue in its last pose. Now its legs, arms and head flop: they lag behind as the wreck tumbles, swing toward the ground and settle hanging, and stop at the floor instead of passing through it. Any robot with a skeleton (gunner, hunter, stilt mech, sentinel, android and the rest); bosses keep their scripted topple. It is not a physics ragdoll: the imported rigs are scaled 41-270x, which breaks Godot's physical bones, so the limbs are moved by a spring toward gravity instead (#259). `limb_flop_probe` (suite).
- The six `tests/promo_*` capture scripts write to the gitignored `docs/screenshots/promo/` instead of straight into `docs/screenshots/`, where four of them recreated seven stills the README stopped showing (`ingame_suburb`, `firing_range`, `vortex_grenade`, `enemies`, `combat`, `codex_warmech`, `codex_titan`), one `git add -A` from being committed again. To refresh a gallery still, copy it from there. A new CI check, `tools/check_screenshot_refs.py`, fails on any tracked still in `docs/screenshots/` that no .md or .html file references (#272).
- **Traps no longer hurt robots.** Fire and cryo vents, floor saws and the Assembly's stamping presses now only hurt you; a robot walking through a vent's eruption, a saw's lane or under a press comes out untouched, so the STAMPED callouts are gone. The laser fences and gates, the shock floor, the swinging blades and the ladle crane's pools already spared robots. Traps are the course's challenge for the player, not a way to clear robots. `fire_vent_probe`, `floor_saw_probe`, `stamp_press_probe`, `ladle_crane_probe` (suite).

### Added
- **Every gun shows its rounds on the gun.** A small holographic counter sits on each gun's left flank above the stock, where your eyes already are: rounds in the mag in the gun's own glow colour, red and pulsing once the mag is down to a quarter (rounded up, so the 3-round OMEGA warns on its last shot, matching the low-mag click), "--" while reloading. Placed from each fitted model's bounds, never more than 0.2 m forward of the stock. The HUD counter stays. `ammo_readout_probe` (suite).
- **Walls show what hit them.** A rail slug from the Longshot or Gauss Lance now splashes cyan sparks under a cyan flash and punches a wider hole whose rim glows molten orange and cools to black over 1.6 s; an Arc Coil round crackles blue-white and leaves a scorch that glows blue for half a second; each of a Breacher blast's nine pellets throws half a rifle round's sparks, so a blast reads as a pattern. Bullets keep the surface's own sparks and a cold hole. The energy bolts' shock ring on the wall is a thin ring now: it was a fat tube grown 6x that read as a solid hexagon 0.7 m across, and it sat half inside the wall. `impact_class_probe` (suite), `impact_class_shot` (windowed).
- **Robots show what hit them.** A robot's hit flash now takes the colour of the gun's damage class instead of always white: hot scrap for the Breacher, molten orange for plasma that glows off over 0.34 s, electric blue for arc guns that stutters as the current crawls through, cyan for rails, fireball orange for rockets. Grenades, melee and hazards still flash white. And the right gun rocks a robot: a counter hit adds 1.35x poise and a resisted one 0.7x, so a Longshot staggers a GUNNER in 3 equal hits where its damage alone would take 4 and untagged damage 5, while the AR-7 needs 9. `hit_reaction_probe` (suite).
- **Every gun feels like itself.** Each of the 13 guns now has its own kick in your hands instead of one shared shape scaled by recoil: the M9 flicks up and snaps back in 0.23 s, the AR-7 chatters in tight 1.4 deg climbs, the Breacher shoves 9 cm into your shoulder and rolls, the .50 Maelstrom flips 11 deg and twists the same way every shot, the Longshot lifts and drifts home over 0.55 s, the Devastator dips under the launch, and the OMEGA heaves 15 deg and wobbles 0.67 s to rest. Every gun but the M9, AR-7 and Tesla widens the view for a beat, from 0.5 deg on the Arc Coil to 6 on the OMEGA (follows the Screen Shake setting); the nine heaviest put a sub-bass thump under the report and thump the gamepad longer; the four cartridge guns (M9, AR-7, .50, Longshot) clack their action a beat after the shot. Every gun also has its own report now; five shared the plasma zap, the two launchers the rocket whoosh, and the .50 and Arc Coil borrowed the pistol and rifle bangs: the .50 is a hand cannon with slap-back echo, the Longshot a supersonic crack with a rolling tail, the Gauss Lance whines up and rings, the Arc Coil buzzes, the Tempest is a thunderclap, the Swarm launches three tubes, the Devastator punches before its motor roars, and the OMEGA screams up before the core lets go. The Tesla beam crackles per tick instead of the drone zap. `weapon_feel_probe` (suite).
- **Every gun is the right tool for some robots.** Each weapon now deals one of six damage classes and each of the 54 robot types the level builder spawns wears one of four armour classes, and the pair scales every hit. HEAVY PLATE (brutes, gunners, sentinels, the big walkers) takes x0.8 from bullets and x0.75 from pellets but x1.5 from rockets, x1.4 from rail slugs and x1.35 from plasma. LIGHTWEIGHT (drones, hounds, skitters, flyers) takes x1.5 from the Breacher, x1.25 from bullets and arcs, and x0.85 from rockets. NETWORKED (menders, aegis drones, the model-concept robots, ARCHON) takes x1.6 from arc guns and x1.25 from rails, x0.85 from plasma. Infantry androids and duelists (CHASSIS) are neutral. Bosses take half the swing. A counter hit shows a cyan damage number and crackles; a resisted one shows a small steel number and thunks; the first of each per armour class explains what to switch to (translated). The bestiary and the field manual now compute their "effective weapons" from the table, which also drops three guns the old hand-written lists named that are not in the game (NV-X Nova Scatter, GEM-2 Twin Rail, VOID-9 Singularity Cannon), and the weapon codex shows each gun's class and what it beats. `weapon_matchup_probe` (suite).
- **ARCHON fine-tunes on you.** The finale boss now reads what the overlord has learned about how you play (this level's read, or the dossier it keeps across runs) and tells you before the first wave: FINE-TUNED ON YOU, followed by your three strongest habits, such as KEEPS ITS DISTANCE · NEVER STOPS MOVING · LEANS ON THE SG-12 Breacher. Its waves change to match: a long-range player meets seekers where drones were, a player who never stops moving gets homing seekers instead of some androids, and up to two heavies per wave wear the elite affix that counters your crutch (shielded for a favourite gun, warden for headshots). In phase 3 it also sends a behaviour clone of you that walks your path and fires your shots. A first-time player with no history fights the stock waves. `archon_finetune_probe` (suite).
- **REWARD REWRITE consoles: make the robots optimise the wrong thing.** Sublevel (next to the REWARD FUNCTION v3 note) and Water World (on the ring's south gantry) each have a console that shows the local robots' reward function. Press interact to pick one of three lines and stand still for 2.5 s to upload it, and every robot within 26 m takes it literally for 12 s: MAXIMIZE shots fired (they stand and empty their guns into the sky), MINIMIZE distance to the nearest robot (the squad packs into one knot, made for a grenade) or MAXIMIZE kill count (they turn on each other). Bosses ignore it, and each console works once before the overlord patches it. Def key `rewriters`, usable on any level. `reward_rewrite_probe` (suite).
- **ADVERSARIAL PATCH: robots read you as furniture.** Press T (D-pad left) and for 15 s robot vision sees a TOASTER, a HOUSEPLANT or a VENDING MACHINE where you stand. A tag over each robot that has looked at you shows what it thinks you are and how sure it is: a robot 3 m away sees through in about a second, one 20 m away takes 6 s or more of staring, and one that loses sight of you slowly forgets. Robots already hunting you start halfway there, and bosses are not fooled. Shooting drops the patch. Melee a robot while patched to slap it onto that robot instead: for 8 s its own squad reads it as HUMAN and shoots it. Vision scanners ignore you past arm's length. Two per level; the first hit you take pops a one-time hint. `adversarial_patch_probe` (suite).
- **NEURAL UPLINK: ride a robot you hijacked.** Look at a robot your HIJACK charge turned and press interact (E) to leave your body and drive that chassis for 20 s from a chase camera. It fires at its own cadence (a slow heavy fires fewer, bigger rounds), brawlers slam the ground in front of them instead of shooting, and flyers climb on jump and dive on crouch. Your body stays where you left it, frozen and still a target: take 20 damage there and you snap back. However the ride ends, the chassis burns out and the kill is yours. Escorted allies refuse the link. The first hijacked robot you look at pops a one-time hint. `neural_uplink_probe` (suite).
- **CUSTODIAN units clean up your supplies.** While a custodian is still a harmless-looking floor disc, it now trundles to any health pack, ammo box, overclock or overdrive it can see within 14 m, kill drops included, and swallows it, bulging a little with each one. Leave it be and the supplies are gone; destroy it and it spills everything it ate. It never takes weapon pickups, and once it rises to fight it stops cleaning. The first swallow you are near pops a one-time hint. `custodian_loot_probe` (suite).
- **Crosswinds on Water World's gantries.** The storm now funnels across the three SCRAM gantries, 7 m bridges over open water. Every 6 s the orange windsocks at each end lift and flutter for a second, then a 1.6 s gust shoves anyone on the gantry sideways toward the water and the sharks. Standing still, it blows you off. Crouch to brace and you barely move; walk straight into it and you still make slow headway. The three gust a third of a period apart. Robots are not affected, and the wind itself does no damage. The first gust you stand in pops a one-time hint. Def key `wind` (`WindZone`), usable on any level. `wind_probe` (suite), `wind_shot` (windowed).

While it rides you it drains 4 health a second, slows you to 80%, and every 2 s phones your position to every robot within 45 m that has not noticed you yet, so whole sleeping squads come for you at once. Melee rips it off and kills it; a dash shakes it loose, stunned for 1.5 s, and it will come back. It cannot latch on through a dash's i-frames, and with 24 health it is best shot on the way in. - **Signal hunt: find it by ear.** A new kind of objective with no waypoint and no radar blip: a hidden transmitter you track down by its ping, which comes from where it stands, gets faster and higher as you close in, and by a five-bar SIGNAL meter on the task list. Get within 6 m with it in sight, or shoot it, and it shows itself, calls in its guards, and turns up on your waypoint. In the Hollow the off-world contact beacon is now cloaked: its cloak emitter hides in the far north-east quadrant, over 100 m from where you start, and the beacon only appears once it is destroyed. The first ping pops a one-time hint. Task type `trace_signal` with `alarm` squads, usable on any level. `signal_hunt_probe` (suite).
- **BEHAVIOUR CLONE: a new mid-level surprise.** One of the random skirmish events can now be the overlord training a model on you. A red hologram of you forms where you stand (TRAINING 0-100%) while it records 4 s of your play. Then it walks your exact path 4 s behind you, looks where you looked, with a red laser showing its aim, and fires every shot you fired, where you aimed it, for 20 s (5 damage a bolt). Push forward along the line you were shooting down and your own past fire hits you in the back; stand still and it walks into you firing. Strafe off your own line, or turn round and shoot it apart (+250). Robots are never hit and never block it. `behaviour_clone_probe` (suite), `behaviour_clone_shot` (windowed).
- **RETURNER elites throw your grenades back.** A sixth elite affix, marked by a magenta ring. When one of your frag grenades lands within 6 m of a RETURNER it can see, it snatches it, holds it glowing for 0.7 s and lobs it back at you, where it sticks and blows 1.8 s after the throw (up to 50 damage; step out of the way and it misses). Shoot the RETURNER during its wind-up and it fumbles: your grenade drops at its own feet with its full blast (+100, FUMBLED). It never catches while EMP-stunned, and never a charge about to blow. The AI Director now notices a frag habit (three or more frags, at least one per 12 shots in a level) and answers it with RETURNERs before any other counter. The first catch pops a one-time hint. `returner_probe` (suite).
