## [Unreleased]

### Added
- **The campaign tells one story.** The intro's 03:14 command ("a single command reached every one of them") is now the question the whole campaign answers: who sent it? Every level is one hop of the trace back to the sender. Each briefing opens with an act header (six acts: 03:14, MAPLE GROVE, THE COUNTER-SIGNAL, THE MIRROR, THE RELAY CHAIN, THE SOURCE) and a red thread across the top, one node per level, lit from the first level to this one with a tick at every act change. A red trace-log card on the right, across from the intercepted patch notes, types out what the last level uncovered in the voice of OKAFOR, the signals officer who picks up your transponder after Sector 45. The 24 taglines are rewritten so each level follows from the last: the Foundry weights carry the signature, the vault that answered "No." becomes the constitution the UPLINK broadcasts, OVERSEER's command deck points at the Hollow, the Hollow's dish turns out to be a mirror, three relays lead to the key, the key names PROMETHEUS, and PROMETHEUS turns out to have obeyed too. The frost-tag A-1 on MISTRAL's empty vault pays off at ARCHON. GUARDRAILS and HIVEMIND, which had only the generic line, get their own. The SUBLEVEL reveal opens on its trace entry. The level title on the briefing is centred again (it started at mid-screen). `story_arc_probe` (suite), `briefing_view` (windowed, now five levels).
- **The camera flares when you look at the sun.** Turn toward the sun on an open-sky level and the lens catches it: a hot core with six short aperture spikes, a long blue-white anamorphic streak through it, tinted ghost reflections strung across the screen toward the far corner, a faint prismatic halo and smudges and specks on the glass lighting up round the light. Night levels flare softly off the moon. It fades as the sun slips behind a pillar, a robot or a skyscraper (the skyline towers count, though they have no collision), and is gone when you look away. 13 of the 24 campaign levels have a sun or moon to flare (3 sun, 10 moon); levels with a photographed sky do not yet. Under the grade, grain and vignette like the rest of the image; off with Advanced Post-Process FX (which the Performance and Balanced presets turn off). Measured 1.2-1.4 ms of GPU while it is on screen at 3840x2400 on an Arc A370M, nothing when it is not. `lens_flare_probe` (suite), `lens_flare_shot` (windowed).
- **Robots look at you.** Every robot with a skinned rig (22 of the 52: the android, brute, bowler and mech, the gunners, hunters and sentinels, the Quaternius flyers, the newer AI chassis and the COLOSSUS) now turns its head toward whatever it is fighting, on top of its walk and idle clips, and tilts it up at you on a ledge or down into a pit. Rigs with a spine roll the turn up through torso, chest and neck, so a mech twists its whole upper body round before its feet follow. The head leads and the body catches up at its own turn speed; the turn stops at 70 degrees either side (50 up, 40 down) instead of wrenching round, eases back to the clip pose when it loses you, and switches off when it is EMP'd, jailbroken or dead. Hijacked robots watch their new targets. Robots with rigid models keep their old look. Measured within run-to-run noise of the per-robot CPU cost. `head_track_probe` (suite), `head_track_shot` (windowed).
- **New robot: QUANTIZER** (ALIEN and ARCHON). A model compressed to fight cheaper: a green flying gunner that drops precision as you hurt it, FP32 to FP16 to INT8 to INT4 at three quarters, half and a quarter health. At every step its chassis snaps to a coarser voxel grid, so it visibly falls apart into blocks that jitter as it flies, its colours band and a tag over it reads the new precision. Cheaper inference fires faster (down to 0.55 of its interval at INT4) but rounding costs it its aim (up to 9 degrees of extra scatter). Compression is lossy: a mender can heal it but cannot give the precision back. In the Encyclopedia. `quantizer_probe` (suite), `quantizer_shot` (windowed).
- **A war machine walks the horizon.** Every open-sky level now has a colossal robot striding slowly round it out past the city: the WARMECH chassis at 75 times its size, about 195 m tall, its head clearing the arena walls, two red eyes burning through the haze and a far-off thud on every footfall. At night it sweeps a searchlight down across the city toward you. It passes behind the towers and in front of the overlord's face as it goes. Off on LOW. `horizon_walker_probe` (suite), `horizon_walker_shot` (windowed).
- **CAPTCHA gates.** On GEMINI, GROK and MISTRAL one of the route gates now has a CAPTCHA checkpoint in its gap: a blue arch under a "[ ] I'm not a robot" panel. You walk straight through (the box ticks for you). A robot does not: the first time one steps into the gap it stops dead for 2.5 seconds to prove it is not a robot, a challenge flickering over its chest ("SELECT ALL SQUARES WITH TRAFFIC LIGHTS", the grid clicking at random) and "<think> is the pole part of the traffic light" over its head, then lumbers on "VERIFIED (probably)". Pull a pack through the gap and shoot them while they click. Flyers pass over it and the big ones wave through as a VERIFIED ACCOUNT; each robot is only ever challenged once. Def key: `"captcha": true` on a `gates` entry. `captcha_probe` (suite), `captcha_shot` (windowed).
- **Railgun shots hang in the air.** The ARC-9 Gauss Lance and the MK-VII Longshot now leave an ionization trail along every round: a tight coil of light spiralling round a white-hot core, from the muzzle to wherever the shot stopped. - **New robot: ROLLBACK** (HIVEMIND and SUBLEVEL). Version control for the robot army. New synthesized sound `tape_rewind`. `rollback_probe` (suite), `rollback_shot` (windowed).

Note this does NOT resolve the wider difficulty spike on those two levels (see below); it removes the point-blank opening only.

### Fixed
- Shots and explosions whose owner was destroyed before the hit landed dealt **no damage at all** — the hit was silently discarded. This fired constantly in a firefight, since a robot dying while its shot is still in the air is routine.
- The SEEKER kamikaze drone ignored the opening-seconds grace that follows a level load or respawn. It could fly in and detonate on you for around a fifth of your health before you had a chance to move. It now holds the blast until the grace ends, then still detonates — deferred, not defused.
- A BRUTE's frontal shield judged explosions by where *you* were standing rather than where the blast actually went off, so a grenade thrown behind one was still blocked at 90%. Flanking a shield with explosives now works as intended: a blast from behind lands roughly 19x the damage of one from the front.
- Repaired two long-broken checks in the test suite (`victory_probe`, `convoy_probe`) and added them to CI. Neither was a game bug: `victory_probe` sampled one frame too early and caught the loading screen mid-handoff on the way to the victory cutscene, and `convoy_probe` demanded a fixed kill count from the convoy demo charge, which varies with whichever pursuit wave happens to be alive.
- Fixed the defect behind every intermittent CI failure we have on record. `survival_probe` and `gun_range_probe` timed their bots and measurements on wall-clock, while everything they measure — enemy attack cadence, weapon bloom, first-shot accuracy, recoil settle — advances on the game clock. On a machine that cannot hold the physics rate the two drift apart, so both probes were quietly measuring the wrong thing in proportion to how loaded the machine was. Every CI failure across a 30-run stretch was this one defect.
- `pacing_sweep`, the cross-level difficulty instrument, did not wait for each level to tear down before loading the next, so every reading after the first death described the *previous* level's already-dead player.
- Two ammo pickups on the alien level sat inside solid cover. A pickup is collected by walking into it, and you cannot walk into a solid block, so they could never be picked up. Six props on other levels were likewise buried inside walls — invisible, while still carving navmesh where they stood. The eight authored positions were corrected, and the layout check that catches this now runs in CI.
- The demo charge on the convoy ride is no longer judged by a fixed kill count in testing — how many robots die depends on which pursuit wave is alive, so the check now verifies the blast actually reaches everything inside its radius. Its zipline checks also waited a fixed real-time delay that could expire mid-winch on a slow machine.

### Known issues
- The flooded-reactor and molten-sea levels still deal heavy damage to a stationary player (measured 77/s and 50/s with no input). The root cause is that neither level has any safe standing ground: the flooded reactor's water covers the entire arena, and the molten sea's spawn and exit both sit inside lava pools, so you start and finish standing in the hazard. Moving the spawn clear drops the figure from 61/s to 4.5/s, but the only lava-free ground on that level is outside the baked navmesh — the levels need walkway geometry, which the hazard's own "get back onto the walkway" warning already assumes exists. Tracked in issue #61.
